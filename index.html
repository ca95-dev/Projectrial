Questions people ask first

How does the try on work?

You upload your photo and a clothing photo. VIRTUFIT places the clothing on your photo and shows a preview.

Is this a full AI try on?

Not yet. This version overlays the clothing flat. A full AI model can be connected later.

Are my photos stored?

No. Everything happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Can I save the result?

Yes. Press Download image after you generate a preview.
